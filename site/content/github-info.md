## Recent updates worth watching

- **Issue statuses and repository boards (public preview):** Use more specific statuses than open/closed to give teams and agents shared context on what is in flight. Source: [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-23-issue-statuses-and-repository-boards-are-in-public-preview).
- **More predictable notifications:** Starting December 1, 2026, GitHub.com notifications migrate over a week to make subscriptions more predictable. Source: [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-24-more-predictable-notifications-on-github-com).
- **Copilot code review billing:** Unless you choose otherwise, reviews for licensed members bill to the organization starting December 30, 2026. Source: [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-25-copilot-code-review-billing-will-default-to-the-organization).
